MA Scan
Stage 2
Daily chart rules (Stage 2 uptrend): (1) Close above SMA 150 and SMA 200. (2) SMA 150 above SMA 200. (3) SMA 200 rising versus 1 month ago. (4) SMA 50 above SMA 150 and SMA 200. (5) Close above SMA 50. (6) Close at least 30% above the 52-week low. (7) Close within 25% of the 52-week high.
Inside Bar
Mother candle = the bigger candle. Baby candle(s) = the latest candle(s) completely inside the mother's high and low. Buy above the Mother High. Breakout option: the previous candle was the inside candle and the latest candle's price is now above its high. Tip: select Stage 2 / Stage 2 VCP + Inside Bar with "All selected (AND)" to get only breakouts of Stage 2 stocks. The mini chart shows the mother, the baby, the buy line and the current price.
Stage 2 VCP (perfect flag)
Daily chart. Rules: (1) Stage 2 uptrend. (2) Strong and smooth prior uptrend. (3) Good volume activity. (4) Orderly pullback, no loose or wide moves. (5) Base not deeper than the max depth. (6) Constructive base: length in range, close above SMA 50 and in the upper half of the base. (7) Young trend: 1st or 2nd flag. (8) Volume contracting from left to right.

Candle colour
Latest candle volume ÷ average volume of the previous N candles (SMA, the latest candle is not included). For a candle that is still forming (15 min, 1 hour, today's Daily) the volume so far is counted, so a spike may appear later in the candle.
